Project 1: Order Data Cleaning & Processing

Data Entry & Data Cleaning Tools: Google Sheets | Excel

Objective

Cleaned and standardized a fictional customer order database merged from several sources: a CRM export, a sales spreadsheet, and manual entries.

What I Did
