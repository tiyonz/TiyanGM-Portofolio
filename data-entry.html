Project 1: Order Data Cleaning & Processing

Data Entry & Data Cleaning Tools: Google Sheets | Excel
Part 1: Objective & Scope of Work Slide 4

Objective

Cleaned and standardized a fictional customer order database merged from several sources: a CRM export, a sales spreadsheet, and manual entries.

What I Did

  • Standardized names, company names, cities, and states.
  • Unified phone numbers (extensions in a separate column), ZIP codes with leading zeros, dates (MM/DD/YYYY), and amounts as numbers.
  • Standardized order statuses and removed duplicate and test records.
  • Fixed email typos only when the correct version appeared elsewhere in the data, and flagged unclear items instead of guessing.

Project 5: US City Research & Data Cleaning

Data Cleaning Tools: Google Sheets | Excel
Part 1: Research, Verification & Metrics Slide 12

Objective

Researched and verified city names in a 100-record dataset, standardized valid US cities, and excluded fictional locations, working on a copy so the raw data stayed intact.

What I Did

  • Researched 62 unique city values using official city sites and Wikipedia.
  • Expanded abbreviations (LA, SF, NYC) and fixed typos and extra spaces.
  • Kept ambiguous names (Springfield, Washington) as is, not guessed.
  • Excluded fictional cities (Gotham, Smallville) from the clean data.
